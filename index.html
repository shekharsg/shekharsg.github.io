Research focus

Understanding how nutrients, water, crops and trade interact to shape sustainable food systems.

I work at the interface of ecohydrology, agricultural nutrient management and environmental systems analysis. My research combines long-term datasets, geospatial analysis, network science and machine learning to quantify environmental pressures and identify pathways toward more resilient food and water systems.

Research areas

Four connected research directions

Explore research →
01

Nutrient pollution & boundaries

Quantifying nitrogen and phosphorus surpluses, efficiencies, spatial disparities and environmental thresholds from district to global scales.

02

Food systems & trade

Tracing physical and virtual environmental burdens through crop trade networks and testing strategies that couple food security with lower impacts.

03

Ecohydrology & water quality

Linking land, water and climate processes to river temperature, water stress, pollution and aquatic ecosystem pressures.

04

Data-driven sustainability

Combining Earth observation, geospatial data, machine learning and environmental accounting to build reproducible decision-support datasets.
