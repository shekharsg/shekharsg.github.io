Biography

I am Dr. Shekhar Sharan Goyal, currently working as a Research Scientist at the Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ), Leipzig, Germany. My work bridges agricultural nutrient management, ecohydrology, and geospatial modeling to promote sustainable and climate-resilient food systems.
