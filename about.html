Biography

I am Dr. Shekhar Sharan Goyal, a Research Scientist at the Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ) in Leipzig, Germany. My work bridges agricultural nutrient management, ecohydrology, environmental accounting and geospatial modelling to understand how food systems can become more sustainable and climate-resilient.

My research has developed from quantifying long-term nitrogen pollution in Indian agriculture, to tracing pollution embedded in interstate crop trade, to evaluating crop-restructuring strategies that jointly consider nutrients, water, greenhouse gases, economics and food security. At UFZ, I am extending this systems perspective through stream-temperature modelling, global nutrient sustainability, water-quality research and AI benchmarking for environmental applications.
