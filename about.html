Dr. Shekhar Sharan Goyal

Research Scientist, UFZ Leipzig, Germany

Brief Biography

I am Dr. Shekhar Sharan Goyal, a Research Scientist at the Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ), Leipzig, Germany. I specialize in agricultural nutrient management, ecohydrology, and sustainable trade networks. My current research focuses on stream-temperature modeling under the FluSeeQ project, aiming to forecast hydrological stress across Germany.
