About Me

I am a Research Scientist at the Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ) in Leipzig, Germany. My work integrates nutrient pollution modeling, ecohydrology, and sustainable agricultural systems. I specialize in the intersection of food, water, and environmental trade-offs, with a focus on data-driven insights for decision support. Currently, I lead stream-temperature modeling in the FluSeeQ project to evaluate climate-driven hydrologic stress across Germany.
