Dr. Shekhar Sharan Goyal

Research Scientist, UFZ Leipzig, Germany

Brief Biography

Dr. Shekhar Sharan Goyal is a Research Scientist at the Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ), Leipzig. His research spans agricultural nutrient management, ecohydrology, and sustainable trade networks. Currently, he leads stream-temperature modeling under the FluSeeQ project to anticipate hydrological stress across Germany.
