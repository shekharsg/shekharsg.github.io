Dr. Shekhar Sharan Goyal

Research Scientist, UFZ Leipzig | Agricultural Sustainability & Ecohydrology

Biography

I am a Research Scientist at the Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ), Leipzig, Germany. My research bridges sustainable agriculture, nutrient pollution modeling, and ecohydrology, with a strong emphasis on environmental policy and trade networks. At UFZ, I lead modeling efforts within the FluSeeQ project to assess stream-temperature dynamics under climate stress across Germany.
